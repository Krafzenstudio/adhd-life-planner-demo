About this planner

This is an organising tool, not a medical device or treatment. It cannot diagnose or treat ADHD. It is designed to help you organise tasks, routines and time in a gentler way.

How to use this planner

Start with one small step. Use Today's three to keep the day contained. Put loose thoughts in Brain Dump and sort them one at a time. Habits can be tiny. Routines have a short version for low-energy days. Close the day to carry unfinished tasks forward without losing them.

This is an organising tool, not a medical device or treatment. It cannot diagnose or treat ADHD.

Take your information with you

A printable copy or CSV can be useful for reviewing your plan. Your data stays on this device unless you export a backup.
